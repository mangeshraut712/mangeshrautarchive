Act I · Philosophy & Architecture

Zero framework runtime. Pure web fundamentals.

Why no React, Angular, or Vue runtime? By relying on native ES modules, vanilla CSS custom properties, and esbuild bundling, we eliminated hydration lag, bloated virtual DOM overhead, and layout shifts. The result is fluid 60–120fps ProMotion scrolling and sub-800ms Largest Contentful Paint.
