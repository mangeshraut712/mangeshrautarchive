## Calculation checks

- OpenRouter illustrative six-turn conversation: prefix 0.048 + new input 0.006 + output 0.012 = USD 0.066 without reuse; warm-prefix path 0.012 + 0.006 + 0.012 = USD 0.030. Saving USD 0.036, approximately 55%. Rates are explicitly hypothetical.
- Retrieval example: 3/5 precision = 60%, 3/10 recall = 30%; wider candidate set 7/12 precision is approximately 58%, 7/10 recall = 70%; deduplicated 7/10 gives 70% for both. Synthetic labels are assumed.
- Serving example: USD 4 / 3,600 accepted tasks × 1,000 = USD 1.11 per 1,000; USD 3 / 2,400 × 1,000 = USD 1.25. These are assumed inputs, not GPU benchmarks.
- Observer selection: 80/100 completion × 72/80 acceptance = 72/100 end-to-end success. Missing outcomes remain visible.
- Notebook weighted average: (100 × 10 + 10 × 30 + 90 × 20) / 200 = 15.5, compared with an unweighted monthly mean of 20. Individual ticket-level conclusions are not identifiable from this aggregate.
- Payments: an 8–10% relative gain from 80% is 86.4–88%; an 8–10 percentage-point gain is 88–90%. The publisher's ambiguous success-rate headline is not assigned an invented denominator.
- Sol illustrative token cost: 10,000 × 2/M + 2,000 × 10/M = USD 0.040; with 8,000 cached input tokens, 2,000 × 2/M + 8,000 × 0.10/M + 2,000 × 10/M = USD 0.0248. Tool, runtime, retry, and review costs are excluded explicitly.
